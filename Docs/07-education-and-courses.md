## 1. Types of courses available in India

| Course type | Duration | Eligibility | Typical fee range (approx.) | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Short-term certificate** (Blender basics, Maya, VFX compositing) | 1–6 months | 10th / 12th / anyone | ₹10,000 – ₹80,000 | Trying it out; skill top-up |
| **Diploma / Advanced diploma** (private academies) | 1–2 years | 10th / 12th | ₹80,000 – ₹3,50,000 | Fast job-oriented training |
| **B.Sc. Animation / B.Sc. Animation & VFX / BVA (Bachelor of Visual Arts) Animation / BA Animation** | 3 years (4 under NEP) | 12th (any stream) | ₹5,000/yr (govt.) – ₹5 lakh/yr (private) | Degree + skills |
| **B.Des (Animation / Animation Film Design / Communication Design)** | 4 years | 12th + design entrance exam (UCEED, NID DAT, institute tests) | ₹1 lakh/yr (NID/IIT, approx.) – ₹5+ lakh/yr (private) | Design-led creative careers |
| **M.Sc. / MA / MVA Animation** | 2 years | Graduation | ₹50,000 – ₹5 lakh/yr | Specialisation |
| **M.Des (Animation)** | 2 years | Graduation + CEED / NID DAT (PG) | Varies | Top-tier design careers |
| **PG Diploma** (Whistling Woods, IICT, etc.) | 1–2 years | Graduation | ₹2 – 10 lakh+ | Industry-ready specialisation |
| **MESC / Skill India certified courses** | Weeks–months | Varies | Low / subsidised | Government-certified skills |
| **Online** (Coursera, Udemy, CG Spectrum, Gnomon Workshop, Blender Studio, YouTube) | Self-paced | Anyone | Free – ₹1 lakh+ | Self-learners, working professionals |

⚠️ **Caution for parents and students:** Quality varies hugely between franchise centres. Before paying:
1. Ask to see **recent student demo reels** (not studio showreels).
2. Ask for **verifiable placement data**: company names and actual salaries, not "up to ₹X LPA".
3. Meet the **faculty** and check their industry experience.
4. Check **lab hardware and software licences**.
5. Read the **refund policy** and avoid paying the full fee upfront where possible.
6. Talk to **alumni** on LinkedIn.

## 5. Entrance exams

| Exam | Conducted by | For | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UCEED** (Undergraduate Common Entrance Exam for Design) | IIT Bombay | B.Des at IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIITDM Jabalpur and participating institutes | Exam in January; registration around Oct–Nov |
| **CEED** (Common Entrance Exam for Design) | IIT Bombay | M.Des (incl. IDC Animation), PhD | Exam in January |
| **NID DAT** (Design Aptitude Test) | NID | B.Des / M.Des at NID campuses | Prelims Dec/Jan; Mains (studio test + interview) Apr–May |
| **NIFT Entrance** | NIFT | Design programmes | Feb |
| **JET** | FTII / SRFTI | Film and animation programmes | Varies |
| **Institute-specific tests + portfolio** | Srishti, Jain, Alliance, Whistling Woods etc. | Their own programmes | Varies |

⚠️ Exam schedules shift each year. Always link to the official exam sites on the website instead of hard-coding dates.

### What design entrance exams test
- Drawing and sketching (perspective, proportion, memory drawing)
- Visual perception and observation
- Creativity and idea generation
- Logical reasoning, general awareness
- Design sensitivity (colour, composition)
- Studio tests: model-making, material handling (NID Mains)

## 7. Choosing the right path: decision guide

```
Are you strong in drawing and want top-tier design careers?
   └─► Prepare for NID DAT / UCEED → B.Des (Animation)

Want a recognised degree at low cost?
   └─► Public university BVA / B.Sc Animation (e.g., Bengaluru City Univ.) + self-learning

Want fast job-readiness and can't do 4 years?
   └─► 1–2 year diploma from a reputed academy (check reels and placements!)

Already a graduate (any stream)?
   └─► PG diploma (IICT, Whistling Woods) / CEED → M.Des / focused online mentorship

Working professional / hobbyist?
   └─► Online (Blender Studio, CG Spectrum, YouTube) + weekend workshops
```
