# 06 — Karnataka and Bengaluru: The State's 3D Animation Ecosystem

> **Why this page matters:** The audience is Indian, with a home base in Karnataka. This is the website's **local differentiator**. No generic global 3D site will cover Karnataka in this depth.

---

## 1. Karnataka at a glance for AVGC

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| **First state AVGC policy in India** | **2012** (Karnataka AVGC Policy). Revised in **2017** and again for **2024–2029** |
| Current policy | **Karnataka AVGC-XR Policy 2024–29** with a proposed outlay of about **₹150 crore** ⚠️ final figures may differ from the draft |
| Job target | **30,000 new high-quality jobs by 2028** (CM Siddaramaiah, Jan 2024). Other reports cite 50,000. At GAFX 2026 the CM projected the sector could generate up to **20 lakh jobs** in 5 years (an aspirational, industry-wide figure) |
| Export target | At least **80% of sector revenue from exports** |
| Flagship event | **Bengaluru GAFX** (Games, Animation, FX), held annually since 2017–18 |
| Industry body | **ABAI: Association of Bangalore Animation Industry** |
| Government agencies | Department of Electronics, IT, Bt and S&T (**Dept. of IT/BT**); **Karnataka Digital Economy Mission (KDEM)**; **Startup Karnataka**; **KITVEN** (venture fund) |
| Infrastructure | **AVGC Centre of Excellence (CoE), Bengaluru**; a proposed **AVGC-XR Park** and a separate gaming CoE |
| Home of | **Hombale Films** (KGF, Kantara, Salaar, *Mahavatar Narsimha*), plus a deep IT/engineering talent base |

## 2. The Karnataka AVGC-XR Policy 2024–29: six pillars

Draft tabled at the 26th Bengaluru Tech Summit (Nov 2023); announced and refined through 2024. ⚠️ Verify the final notified version on the Karnataka IT/BT department website before publishing exact numbers.

| # | Pillar | What it means for students and studios |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Skill development: a future-ready workforce** | Curriculum input via a **Skill Advisory Committee**; reimbursements for skilling; links with colleges |
| 2 | **Sustainable infrastructure** | Expanded AVGC CoE; separate Gaming CoE; **AVGC-XR Park** (proposed as an SEZ-style hub) with virtual production stages, shared equipment and hackathon spaces |
| 3 | **Empowering startups and MSMEs** | **KITVEN AVGC Fund**: early-stage investment of about **₹0.5–2 crore per startup** ⚠️ |
| 4 | **Global market access and business development** | Support for international markets (MIPCOM, Annecy, GDC, SIGGRAPH), B2B meets at GAFX |
| 5 | **Incentives, concessions and financial support** | Capital subsidies, rental support, IP-creation incentives ⚠️ check the final policy text |
| 6 | **"Mobile First" AVGC products** | Focus on mobile games, apps and short-form content, which matches India's mobile-first users |

### The AVGC Centre of Excellence (Bengaluru)
- A state-supported facility giving **startups and students access to high-end tools, render capacity, motion capture, post-production infrastructure, mentoring and global networks**.
- Run in partnership with industry (ABAI). ⚠️ Confirm the current address, operator and access process before listing.

> **Bengaluru's edge:** Compared with Mumbai (film and content-heavy) and Hyderabad (VFX and gaming-heavy), Bengaluru combines **creative talent with deep technical and engineering talent**. That is ideal for **real-time 3D, game engines, XR, technical direction, pipeline development and AI tooling**.

## 7. Kannada and Karnataka culture as 3D animation material

This is a major creative opportunity, and a strong emotional hook for the website.

| Tradition | Animation potential |
|---|---|
| **Togalu Gombeyaata** (Karnataka's leather shadow puppetry) | An ancient form of moving-image storytelling, an "ancestor" of animation. Great for a history section and stylised NPR 3D looks |
| **Yakshagana** | Elaborate costumes, crowns and dramatic make-up; stunning 3D character design and dance mocap |
| **Mysuru Dasara** | Jamboo Savari elephants, illuminated palace; ideal for 3D environments and crowd simulation |
| **Channapatna toys** | Lacquered wooden toys (GI tag) with simple shapes and bright colours. A perfect "low-poly / stylised" art direction and a great brand motif for the website |
| **Kinnal craft** (Koppal) | Traditional painted wooden figurines |
| **Mysuru painting** | Gold-leaf traditional painting style; could inspire stylised shaders and textures |
| **Ganjifa cards** (Mysuru) | Circular painted playing cards; motion-graphics inspiration |
| **Hampi, Belur, Halebidu, Pattadakal, Badami, Aihole** | Hoysala and Chalukya architecture; photogrammetry, VR heritage walks, game environments |
| **Folk tales**: *Punyakoti / Govina Haadu*, tales of **Tenali Rama** (Vijayanagara court), **Kittur Rani Chennamma**, **Onake Obavva**, **Sangolli Rayanna**, **Basavanna** and the Vachana tradition | Strong Kannada-rooted IP opportunities for animated films and series |
| **Bhoota Kola, Kambala, Huli Vesha** (coastal Karnataka) | Visually spectacular; *Kantara* showed mass appeal |

### The Kannada-language opportunity
- Most Indian animation is produced in Hindi/English and **dubbed** into Kannada (e.g., POGO's *Rise of Anshaks* and *Mahavatar Narsimha* include Kannada versions).
- There are **very few original Kannada animated series or features**. That gap is an opportunity for creators, and a clear storytelling angle for the website ("Kannada stories, told in 3D").
- Kannada YouTube animation (rhymes, folk tales, devotional content) has a large audience.
