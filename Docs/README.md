## Key facts at a glance (for the hero section and highlights)

- **India's Media & Entertainment sector** grew **9% to ₹2.78 trillion in 2025** and is projected to reach **₹3.3 trillion by 2028** (FICCI-EY, March 2026). Animation & VFX grew a modest **2% in 2025** but is named a primary growth driver through 2028.
- **Mahavatar Narsimha (2025)** — presented by Bengaluru-based **Hombale Films** — became **India's highest-grossing animated film ever**: about **₹326 crore gross worldwide** on a budget of about **₹40 crore**. It was the first animated film (Indian or foreign) to cross ₹100 crore in India, and it was among the 35 films eligible for the Best Animated Feature Oscar at the 98th Academy Awards.
- **Karnataka was the first Indian state to launch an AVGC policy (2012)**. Its current **AVGC-XR Policy 2024–29** has an outlay of about **₹150 crore**.
- **Bengaluru GAFX** (7th edition: 27 Feb – 1 Mar 2026) is Karnataka's annual animation, VFX, gaming and XR conference.
- **IICT Mumbai** (Indian Institute of Creative Technologies) is India's national Centre of Excellence for AVGC-XR, modelled on IITs/IIMs, and has been running since July 2025.
- **Blender**, a professional 3D tool, is **free**. Anyone in India with a mid-range PC can start learning today.

## Reliability note

Figures on market size, salaries and fees vary a lot between sources. Throughout these docs:

- ✅ **Verified**: the figure comes from an official or primary source (government, EY, studio announcement).
- ⚠️ **Indicative**: the figure comes from aggregators, course providers or press estimates. Show it on the website as a range and add "approximate".

Before launch, re-check every number marked ⚠️, especially fees, salaries and software prices, which change every year.
