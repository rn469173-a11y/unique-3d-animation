# 02 — History of 3D Animation: World and India

## Part A: Global timeline

| Year | Milestone | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1960s | **William Fetter** (Boeing) coins the term "computer graphics" and creates a wireframe human figure | Birth of CG |
| 1963 | **Ivan Sutherland's Sketchpad** (MIT) | First interactive computer graphics program; the ancestor of all 3D software |
| 1972 | **"A Computer Animated Hand"** by Ed Catmull and Fred Parke (University of Utah) | One of the first 3D-rendered animations. Catmull later co-founded Pixar |
| 1975 | **Utah Teapot** created by Martin Newell | Still the standard 3D test model; a famous in-joke in the industry |
| 1976 | *Futureworld*, the first feature film to use 3D CGI (Catmull's hand and face) | CGI enters cinema |
| 1982 | ***Tron*** (Disney) | Extensive computer-generated sequences |
| 1984 | *The Adventures of André & Wally B.* (Lucasfilm Computer Division) | Introduced motion blur and squash-and-stretch to CG characters |
| 1986 | **Pixar founded** (Steve Jobs buys Lucasfilm's graphics group); ***Luxo Jr.*** | Lamp character becomes Pixar's logo; Oscar nomination |
| 1988 | *Tin Toy* (Pixar) | First CG film to win an Oscar (Animated Short) |
| 1989 | *The Abyss*: the water "pseudopod" | Photoreal CG in live action |
| 1991 | *Terminator 2*: liquid-metal T-1000 | VFX landmark |
| 1993 | ***Jurassic Park*** | Photorealistic CG creatures; changed VFX forever |
| 1995 | ***Toy Story***, the first fully computer-animated feature film | The start of the 3D animated feature era |
| 1998 | **Maya 1.0** released (Alias\|Wavefront); *Antz* and *A Bug's Life* | Maya became the industry standard |
| 2001 | *Shrek*, *Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within*, *Monsters, Inc.*; Gollum in *Lord of the Rings* | First Oscar for Best Animated Feature (*Shrek*, 2002 ceremony); performance-capture breakthrough |
| 2002 | **Blender goes open-source** | Free professional 3D for everyone, a big deal for students in India |
| 2004 | *The Polar Express* | First all-performance-capture feature |
| 2009 | ***Avatar*** | Stereoscopic 3D and performance capture at scale |
| 2013 | *Frozen*; Pixar's RenderMan moves to physically based rendering | Realistic snow and cloth; PBR becomes standard |
| 2014 | *Big Hero 6* (Disney's Hyperion path-tracer) | Modern path-traced rendering |
| 2018 | ***Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse*** | Stylised NPR 3D goes mainstream |
| 2019 | *The Mandalorian* uses LED-wall virtual production with Unreal Engine | Real-time game engines enter filmmaking |
| 2019 | *The Lion King* (2019) | Fully photoreal "live-action" animated film; VFX by MPC, with large teams in Bengaluru and Mumbai |
| 2021 | *Arcane* (Fortiche) | Painterly 3D/2D hybrid TV series |
| 2023 | Generative AI tools (text-to-image, text-to-video) emerge | Pipeline disruption begins |
| 2025 | Technicolor Group (parent of MPC, Mikros, The Mill) collapses | Thousands of jobs lost globally, including in Bengaluru and Mumbai |
| 2026 (Mar) | ***KPop Demon Hunters*** (Netflix / Sony Pictures Animation) wins Best Animated Feature at the 98th Oscars | Streaming-first animated films win top honours |

## Part B: History of animation in India

### Early era (1930s–1980s): 2D roots
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1934 | ***On a Moonlit Night***, credited as one of India's earliest animated films (Gunamoy Banerjee) |
| 1956 | **Films Division of India** sets up a cartoon unit with help from Disney animator **Clair Weeks** |
| 1957 | ***The Banyan Deer*** (Films Division) |
| 1968+ | **Ram Mohan**, the "Father of Indian Animation", joins Films Division and later founds Ram Mohan Biographics (1972) and Graphiti |
| 1974 | ***Ek Anek Aur Ekta*** (Films Division, dir. Vijaya Mulay), the famous Doordarshan short on unity in diversity |
| 1986 | ***Ghayab Aya*** (Doordarshan), considered India's first animated TV series |

### The outsourcing and TV boom (1990s–2000s)
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1992 | ***Ramayana: The Legend of Prince Rama*** (Indo-Japanese co-production; Ram Mohan was co-director) |
| 1990s | Cable TV arrives; Cartoon Network and Nickelodeon create demand. Studios like **Pentamedia Graphics** (Chennai), **Toonz** (Thiruvananthapuram), **Crest Animation** and **UTV Toons** begin outsourcing work for global studios |
| 1999–2000 | **Tata Elxsi's Visual Computing Labs (VCL)** and **Pentamedia** pioneer Indian 3D/CGI. Pentamedia made *Pandavas: The Five Warriors* (2000), a fully 3D Indian feature that won a National Award |
| 2005 | ***Hanuman*** (2D), the first Indian animated feature to be a commercial success, sparking a mythology-animation wave |
| 2007 | *My Friend Ganesha*, *Bal Ganesh* (Shemaroo) |
| 2008 | ***Roadside Romeo*** (Yash Raj Films + Disney; animated by Tata Elxsi VCL), the first big-banner Bollywood 3D animated feature |
| 2008 | ***Chhota Bheem*** (Green Gold Animation, Hyderabad) launches on POGO and becomes India's most popular kids' character |
| 2010 | ***Toonpur Ka Superrhero*** (Ajay Devgn; live-action/animation hybrid) |
| 2012 | ***Delhi Safari*** (Krayon Pictures), a 3D feature that won the National Award for Best Animated Film |
| 2012 | ***Arjun: The Warrior Prince*** (UTV/Disney) |
| 2012 | ***Motu Patlu*** (Cosmos-Maya), later in 3D; one of Nickelodeon India's biggest hits |
| 2012 | **Karnataka launches India's first state AVGC policy** |
| 2014 | ***Kochadaiiyaan*** (Rajinikanth), India's first full motion-capture feature |
| 2014 | **Prime Focus merges with Double Negative → DNEG**, making an Indian-owned company one of the world's top VFX houses |

### The VFX and streaming era (2015–2024)
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 2015 / 2017 | ***Baahubali*** 1 & 2, a turning point for Indian VFX ambition |
| 2016 | DNEG wins Oscar for *Ex Machina* VFX (after *Interstellar*, 2015). Indian teams contributed to its later Oscars for *First Man*, *Tenet*, *Dune* and *Dune: Part Two* |
| 2018 | ***Little Singham*** (Reliance Animation) launches on Discovery Kids |
| 2021 | ***The Legend of Hanuman*** (Graphic India) launches on Disney+ Hotstar, a premium mythological 3D series for young adults |
| 2022 | ***Brahmastra*** (DNEG + ReDefine); ***RRR*** (VFX by several Indian studios); ***KGF Chapter 2*** (Hombale Films, Bengaluru) |
| 2022 | **Union Budget announces the AVGC Promotion Task Force** |
| 2024 | ***Kalki 2898 AD***, a big-budget sci-fi heavy on VFX |
| 2024 | **IICT (Indian Institute of Creative Technologies)** announced as the national AVGC-XR Centre of Excellence in Mumbai |

### The breakout era (2025–2026)
| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Feb 2025 | **Technicolor India shuts down** suddenly; roughly 2,000–3,200 employees in Bengaluru and Mumbai are affected (MPC, Mikros Animation, Technicolor Games) |
| Mar 2025 | Government announces a **US$1 billion fund for the creator economy** |
| May 2025 | **WAVES 2025** (World Audio Visual & Entertainment Summit), Mumbai. IICT confirmed at Film City, Goregaon, with a one-time grant of about ₹391 crore; over US$1.1 billion in deals reported |
| Jul 2025 | **IICT begins operations** (initially at BKC, Mumbai) |
| 25 Jul 2025 | ***Mahavatar Narsimha*** releases (Hombale Films / Kleem Productions; dir. Ashwin Kumar) in 3D, in five languages including Kannada. Becomes **India's highest-grossing animated film**: about **₹326.8 crore gross worldwide** (Sacnilk) |
| Nov 2025 | *Mahavatar Narsimha* is among the **35 films eligible** for the Best Animated Feature Oscar (98th Academy Awards). It was not among the final five nominees |
| Feb–Mar 2026 | **Bengaluru GAFX 2026** (7th edition), themed "Evolution Reloaded" |
| Apr 2026 | First look of ***Mahavatar Parshuram***, the second film in the Mahavatar Cinematic Universe (slated for December 2027) |
| Oct 2026 | *The Legend of Hanuman* Season 7 on JioHotstar; POGO's *Rise of Anshaks* (Chhota Bheem × Little Singham crossover) |

## Pioneers worth featuring (Indian)

| Person | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Ram Mohan** (1931–2019) | "Father of Indian Animation"; Padma Shri; trained generations of animators |
| **Bhimsain Khurana** | Films Division director; *Ek Anek Aur Ekta* animation, *Ghayab Aya* |
| **Rajiv Chilaka** | Founder of Green Gold Animation; creator of *Chhota Bheem* |
| **P. Jayakumar** | Founder of Toonz Media Group |
| **Namit Malhotra** | Founder of Prime Focus; Chairman/CEO of DNEG; producer of the *Ramayana* (2026–27) films |
| **Ketan Mehta** | Founder of Maya Entertainment (now MAAC); early animation education in India |
| **Ashwin Kumar** | Director of *Mahavatar Narsimha* |
| **Vijay Kiragandur** | Founder of **Hombale Films** (Bengaluru), producer of KGF, Kantara and the Mahavatar universe |
| **Pete Draper** | Co-founder of Makuta VFX (Hyderabad); *Baahubali*, *Eega* |
| **Sharad Devarajan** | Co-founder of Graphic India; creator of *The Legend of Hanuman* |
