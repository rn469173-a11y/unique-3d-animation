# 10 — Trends and the Future of 3D Animation (2026 and Beyond)

## 1. Generative AI in animation and VFX

### What AI is already doing (2025–2026)
| Area | AI capability | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Rotoscoping and cleanup** | Automatic subject isolation, wire removal | Nuke CopyCat, Runway, Adobe tools, SAM-based tools |
| **Matchmove and tracking** | Faster camera solves | ML-assisted trackers |
| **Motion capture** | Markerless mocap from phone or webcam video | Move.ai, Rokoko Vision, DeepMotion, Plask |
| **In-betweening and rigging** | Auto-rigging, motion in-betweening | Cascadeur, AccuRIG, studio tools |
| **Text/image-to-3D** | Generate rough 3D assets from prompts or images | Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, Luma, Hunyuan3D |
| **Texturing** | AI-generated textures and materials | Substance 3D (Firefly), Stable Diffusion workflows |
| **AI video generation** | Text-to-video, image-to-video | Runway, Kling, Veo, Sora, Pika, etc. |
| **Previs and concept** | Rapid visual exploration | Midjourney, Firefly, Stable Diffusion |
| **Denoising and upscaling** | Faster clean renders | NVIDIA OptiX, Intel OIDN, DLSS |
| **Lip-sync and dubbing** | Auto lip-sync for multilingual versions, which matters for India's many languages | AI dubbing / lip-sync tools |

### Impact on Indian jobs (balanced view)
- **Most exposed:** repetitive tasks that India has traditionally serviced for Hollywood, such as **roto, paint, matchmove, cleanup** and basic in-betweening.
- **Projected efficiency:** a FICCI-EY 2024 projection suggests AI could raise studio efficiency up to **3×** and add **10–15% EBITDA** gains through automation ⚠️ (projection).
- **New roles emerging:** *AI pipeline artist, generative content lead, ML-tools TD, prompt-to-previs artist, AI supervisor* (deciding where AI fits and where it doesn't).
- **Global studios still investing in India:** e.g., **Netflix's Eyeline Studios in Hyderabad**, and DNEG's Indian expansion.
- **Government stance:** IICT has a **National Creator Lab** roadmap with **Adobe** (LoI, Feb 2026). Karnataka's CM said at GAFX 2026 that **"AI must empower, not replace, human creativity."**

### Advice for students (put this on the website)
1. **Learn fundamentals deeply**: storytelling, acting, anatomy, lighting and design can't be automated.
2. **Learn to use AI tools** as accelerators, not replacements.
3. **Move up the value chain**: from roto to compositing, from modelling to look-dev, from animation to direction.
4. **Learn technical skills**: Python, Houdini, Unreal. Hybrid artist-technologists are the most future-proof.
5. **Build original IP**: your own stories and characters.
6. **Ethics and copyright**: understand licensing of AI training data; many studios restrict generative AI on client work.

## 2. Real-time rendering and game engines

- **Unreal Engine 5** (Lumen lighting, Nanite geometry, MetaHuman) is used for **animated series, previs, cinematics and virtual production**, not just games.
- Real-time means **instant feedback** in place of overnight renders, which cuts costs.
- Blender **EEVEE Next** and Unity HDRP are also used for stylised productions.
- **Indian relevance:** Bengaluru's engineering talent and gaming studios are well-placed here.

## 3. Virtual production (VP)

- Actors perform in front of **giant LED walls** showing real-time 3D environments (popularised by *The Mandalorian*, 2019).
- India has growing VP stages in Mumbai, Hyderabad and Chennai. Karnataka's **AVGC-XR Park** plan includes **virtual production facilities**.
- New roles: **VP supervisor, Unreal environment artist, LED-volume technician, real-time compositor**.

## 4. USD and open standards

- **OpenUSD (Universal Scene Description)**, created by Pixar and now governed by the **Alliance for OpenUSD (AOUSD)** (Pixar, Apple, Adobe, Autodesk, NVIDIA and others), is becoming the universal 3D file standard across film, games, XR and industrial digital twins.
- **glTF / GLB** is the "JPEG of 3D" for the web and AR.
- **MaterialX, OpenColorIO, OpenEXR** are part of the open-source ecosystem (Academy Software Foundation).

## 5. Stylised and NPR animation boom

- After *Spider-Verse*, *Arcane*, *Puss in Boots: The Last Wish* and *KPop Demon Hunters* (Oscar winner, March 2026), audiences love **stylised looks**.
- **Opportunity for India:** styles drawn from **Mysuru painting, Madhubani, Warli, Pattachitra, Kalamkari, Tanjore, Togalu Gombeyaata and Channapatna toys** could create a unique Indian visual language in 3D.

## 6. XR: AR, VR and mixed reality

- **AR** in e-commerce (try-on of glasses, jewellery and furniture), education and tourism (heritage sites).
- **VR** training (medical, defence, industrial), immersive storytelling, VR museums.
- Devices: Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest, Android XR.
- **3D on the web:** Three.js, Babylon.js, WebGPU, `<model-viewer>`, and Gaussian splatting viewers.

## 7. Gaussian splatting and neural rendering

- **3D Gaussian Splatting (2023+)** turns photos and video into photoreal 3D scenes viewable in real time.
- Big potential for **heritage digitisation** (Hampi, Belur), real-estate tours and film backgrounds.
- **NeRFs** (Neural Radiance Fields) are a related technique.

## 8. Cloud and remote pipelines

- Cloud workstations and render farms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, specialised farms).
- **Remote/hybrid work** is normal since 2020, which helps artists in tier-2 cities like Mysuru, Hubballi and Mangaluru.
