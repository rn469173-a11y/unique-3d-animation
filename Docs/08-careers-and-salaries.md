# 08 — Careers and Salaries in 3D Animation (India)

> ⚠️ **Salary data in India is fragmented.** Figures below combine Payscale, AAFT, Careers360 and general industry ranges as of 2026. They vary heavily by **city, studio, project type (film/TV/ads/games/VFX), software skills and portfolio quality**. Present them on the website as **ranges**, never as guarantees.

---

## 1. Career map: who does what

### Pre-production
| Role | What they do | Key skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Concept Artist** | Designs the look of characters, worlds and props | Drawing, painting, Photoshop/Krita/Procreate, design sense |
| **Storyboard Artist** | Turns the script into visual shot sequences | Drawing, cinematic language, storytelling |
| **Visual Development (Vis-dev) Artist** | Colour scripts, mood, style exploration | Painting, colour theory |
| **Previs Artist** | Rough 3D blocking of scenes and cameras | Maya / Blender / Unreal, cinematography |

### Production: asset creation
| Role | What they do | Key skills |
|---|---|---|
| **3D Modeller** (character / environment / prop / hard-surface) | Builds 3D models | Maya, Blender, 3ds Max, ZBrush, topology |
| **Digital Sculptor** | High-detail organic sculpts | ZBrush, anatomy |
| **Texture Artist** | Paints surface maps | Substance 3D, Mari, Photoshop |
| **Look-dev / Shading Artist** | Builds materials and shaders | Arnold, RenderMan, V-Ray, Karma, node networks |
| **Rigging Artist / Character TD** | Builds skeletons and controls | Maya rigging, Python, anatomy, problem-solving |
| **Groom Artist** | Hair and fur | XGen, Yeti, Houdini, Blender curves |

### Production: shots
| Role | What they do | Key skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Layout Artist** | Sets up cameras and staging per shot | Cinematography, Maya/Blender |
| **3D Animator** (character / creature / technical) | Brings characters to life | 12 principles, acting, timing, Maya/Blender |
| **Motion-capture Artist / Cleanup** | Processes and cleans mocap data | MotionBuilder, Maya |
| **FX Artist / FX TD** | Fire, water, smoke, destruction, magic | **Houdini**, VEX, physics |
| **CFX Artist** | Cloth and hair simulation | Maya nCloth, Houdini Vellum, Marvelous Designer |
| **Crowd Artist** | Large crowds | Golaem, Houdini |
| **Lighting Artist** | Lights shots, creates mood | Lighting theory, renderers, photography sense |

### Post-production / VFX
| Role | What they do | Key skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Roto Artist** (often the **entry-level job**) | Cuts out objects and actors frame by frame | Silhouette, Nuke, patience and precision |
| **Paint / Prep Artist** | Removes wires, rigs and blemishes | Nuke, Silhouette |
| **Matchmove / Tracking Artist** | Recreates real camera motion in 3D | 3DEqualizer, PFTrack, SynthEyes |
| **Compositor** | Integrates everything into the final image | **Nuke**, After Effects, colour science |
| **Colourist** | Final colour grading | DaVinci Resolve |

### Technical / pipeline
| Role | What they do | Key skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline TD / Pipeline Developer** | Builds tools that connect departments | **Python**, C++, USD, databases |
| **R&D / Graphics Programmer** | Develops renderers, simulation, AI tools | C++, CUDA, maths, ML |
| **Render Wrangler / Systems Engineer** | Manages render farms | Linux, networking, scripting |
| **Technical Artist** (games) | Bridges art and engineering | Unreal/Unity, shaders, Python/C# |

### Real-time, games and XR
| Role | Key skills |
|---|---|
| **Game 3D Artist** (characters / environments / props) | Low-poly and high-poly workflow, baking, Substance, Unreal/Unity |
| **Game Animator** | Game-ready loops, state machines, Unreal animation blueprints |
| **Unreal Engine Artist / Virtual Production Artist** | Unreal, Lumen/Nanite, LED volume workflows |
| **AR/VR Developer / 3D Web Developer** | Unity/Unreal, **Three.js / WebGL / WebXR**, glTF optimisation |

### Leadership and production
- **Lead / Supervisor** (Animation Lead, CG Supervisor, VFX Supervisor)
- **Animation Director / Film Director**
- **Production Coordinator → Production Manager → Line Producer → Producer**
- **Art Director / Production Designer**

### Allied careers
- **ArchViz Artist** (architecture and interiors: huge in Indian real estate)
- **Product Visualisation Artist** (e-commerce, ads)
- **Motion Graphics Designer** (broadcast, IPL/sports graphics, YouTube)
- **Medical / Scientific Animator**
- **3D Printing / Toy Designer**
- **Animation Educator / Trainer** (high demand from colleges and IICT's Train-the-Trainer)
- **YouTuber / Content Creator / Freelancer** (Fiverr, Upwork, ArtStation)

## 2. Salary ranges in India (2026, indicative) ⚠️

### By experience (3D animation / VFX in general)
| Level | Experience | Annual CTC (approx.) | Monthly (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intern / Trainee** | 0 | Unpaid – ₹1.8 lakh | ₹0 – 15,000 stipend |
| **Fresher / Junior Artist** | 0–1 yr | **₹2.4 – 4.8 lakh** | ₹20,000 – 40,000 |
| **Junior–Mid Artist** | 1–3 yrs | **₹4 – 8.5 lakh** | ₹35,000 – 70,000 |
| **Mid-level / Senior Artist** | 3–6 yrs | **₹8 – 16 lakh** | ₹65,000 – 1.3 lakh |
| **Lead / Supervisor** | 6–10 yrs | **₹15 – 30 lakh** | ₹1.25 – 2.5 lakh |
| **Head of Department / VFX Supervisor / Director** | 10+ yrs | **₹30 lakh – 1 crore+** | — |

*Sources: AAFT (fresher ₹3.0–4.8L, junior ₹5.0–8.5L, mid ₹9–16L); Payscale early-career 3D animator average ~₹2.9–3.05 lakh base (Delhi/Hyderabad, 2026); Careers360 ~₹25,000/month for freshers. Upper-level ranges are general industry estimates.*

### By role (relative pay, mid-level)
| Higher pay ↑ | Mid pay | Entry / lower pay ↓ |
|---|---|---|
| Pipeline TD, R&D, FX TD (Houdini), Rigging TD, Technical Artist, Unreal / Real-time specialists, Graphics programmers | Character Animator, Lighting, Look-dev, Compositor, Game Artist | Roto, Paint, Matchmove (entry), Junior Modeller |

> **Key insight for Indian students:** **Technical-creative hybrid roles** (Houdini FX, rigging, pipeline/Python, Unreal, technical art) are the best-paid and hardest to automate. Bengaluru's tech ecosystem makes them especially relevant here.

### By city (relative)

## 3. Career paths (typical progression)

```
Animation track:  Trainee → Junior Animator → Animator → Senior Animator → Lead → Animation Supervisor → Animation Director
VFX track:        Roto/Paint → Compositor → Senior Compositor → Comp Lead → Comp Supervisor → VFX Supervisor
Technical track:  Junior TD → TD → Senior TD → Pipeline Lead / Head of Pipeline → CTO
Games track:      Junior 3D Artist → 3D Artist → Senior → Lead Artist → Art Director
Production track: Coordinator → Production Manager → Line Producer → Producer → Head of Studio
```

## 4. What studios look for (hiring checklist)

1. **Demo reel / portfolio** (ArtStation, Vimeo, YouTube). By far the most important. See [11-learning-roadmap.md](11-learning-roadmap.md).
2. **Fundamentals**: anatomy, perspective, timing, lighting, composition.
3. **Software proficiency** in industry tools (Maya, Houdini, Nuke, Unreal, Substance, Blender).
4. **Specialisation**: be excellent at one thing rather than average at everything.
5. **Studio test**: most Indian studios give a practical test.
6. **Soft skills**: taking feedback, meeting deadlines, communication, teamwork.
7. **Basic Python** is a growing advantage in every department.
8. **Language**: working English for international clients.

## 5. Realities to be honest about (build trust with parents)

- **Entry-level pay can be modest** and hours long during deadlines ("crunch").
- **Service work is project-based**: layoffs happen when projects end (e.g., Technicolor 2025).
- **Growth is fast for skilled people**: a strong artist can double their salary in 2–3 years.
- **AI is changing junior work** (roto, cleanup), so keep upskilling.
- **Portfolio beats pedigree**: a great reel from a small college beats a weak reel from a famous one.
- **Contracts**: read them carefully. Check notice periods, bonds and IP clauses.
