## 1. Spotlight: *Mahavatar Narsimha* (2025)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Director** | Ashwin Kumar |
| **Presented by** | **Hombale Films** (Bengaluru, Karnataka) |
| **Produced by** | Kleem Productions |
| **Release** | 25 July 2025, theatrical in **3D** |
| **Languages** | Hindi, **Kannada**, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam |
| **Story** | Based on the legend of Prahlad and Lord Vishnu's **Narasimha avatar**, which destroys the demon king Hiranyakashipu |
| **Budget** | About **₹40 crore** |
| **Box office** | About **₹326.82 crore gross worldwide** (India gross ~₹298.82 crore + overseas ~₹28 crore; net ~₹251.30 crore), per Sacnilk |
| **Records** | **First animated film (Indian or foreign) to cross ₹100 crore in India**, reached in about 12 days. **Highest-grossing animated film in India**, beating Hollywood titles. Previous Indian animation benchmarks: *Hanuman* (2005) ~₹10 crore domestic; *Kochadaiiyaan* (2014) ~₹30 crore worldwide (as reported) |
| **Run** | Slow opening (~₹2.29 crore day 1) that grew through **word of mouth and family audiences**; 50+ days in theatres, then streaming on **Netflix** |
| **Oscars** | One of **35 films eligible** for Best Animated Feature at the 98th Academy Awards (after completing a qualifying US theatrical run). Not among the final 5 nominees. The winner was *KPop Demon Hunters* |
| **Franchise** | Launches the **Mahavatar Cinematic Universe**: a slate of films on Vishnu's avatars running to *Mahavatar Kalki Part 2* (2037). Next: ***Mahavatar Parshuram*** (slated **December 2027**) |

**Why it matters:**
1. It proved **Indian audiences will pay for Indian animated films** in theatres, not only kids' TV.
2. It showed a **mid-budget (₹40 crore) Indian film can earn about 8× its cost**.
3. It was **multilingual from day one**, a pan-India release strategy borrowed from KGF and Baahubali.
4. It has a **Karnataka connection**: Hombale Films, the Bengaluru production house behind KGF and Kantara.

## 5. Indians in global animation and VFX

- **DNEG**, an Indian-owned company (Prime Focus / Namit Malhotra), has won multiple **VFX Oscars**: *Inception* (pre-merger), *Interstellar*, *Ex Machina*, *First Man*, *Tenet*, *Dune*, *Dune: Part Two*. Indian teams contribute heavily to its films.
- Indian artists work at **Pixar, DreamWorks, Disney, ILM, Weta FX, Framestore, Sony Pictures Imageworks** and others.
- **Indian-origin creatives in Hollywood animation:** e.g., **Sanjay Patel** (Pixar; director of the Oscar-nominated short ***Sanjay's Super Team***, 2015), **Ashwin Kumar** (Hollywood-trained). Verify any further names before publishing.
- **Hollywood films serviced from Bengaluru** (through MPC/Technicolor, DNEG and others) include *The Lion King* (2019), *The Jungle Book* (2016), *Sonic*, and many Marvel and DC titles. ⚠️ Credit lists span multiple locations; phrase as "Indian teams contributed to…".
