# 01 — What is 3D Animation?

## 1. Simple definition

**3D animation** is the process of creating moving images in a **three-dimensional digital space**. Artists build objects and characters on a computer as 3D models (with height, width and depth), give them surfaces and colours, place lights and cameras around them, make them move over time, and then **render** (calculate) the final images frame by frame.

> **One-line version for the website:** *3D animation is digital puppetry: you build a puppet on the computer, give it bones, move it, light it like a film set, and let the computer photograph every frame.*

## 2. How it works: the basic idea

1. **Modelling**: build the shape out of a mesh of points (vertices), edges and faces (polygons).
2. **Texturing and shading**: give the surface its colour, roughness, shine and material (skin, metal, cloth).
3. **Rigging**: put a digital skeleton (bones and controls) inside the model so it can move.
4. **Animation**: pose the rig at key moments (**keyframes**) and the software fills in the motion between them.
5. **Lighting and camera**: place virtual lights and cameras, just like on a film set.
6. **Rendering**: the computer calculates how light hits every surface and produces final images. At 24 frames per second, a 90-minute film is about **1,29,600 frames**.
7. **Compositing**: combine the layers, add effects and colour-grade.

The full pipeline is explained in [03-production-pipeline.md](03-production-pipeline.md).

## 3. 2D vs 3D vs Stop-motion

| Feature | 2D Animation | 3D Animation | Stop-motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| How it's made | Drawn frame by frame (paper or digital) | Built and moved in 3D software | Real physical puppets photographed frame by frame |
| Depth | Flat; depth is an illusion | True depth; the camera can move anywhere | Real depth |
| Re-use | Each frame drawn again | Models and rigs re-used across scenes and seasons | Puppets re-used |
| Look | Hand-drawn, stylised | Anything from cartoon to photorealistic | Tactile, handmade |
| Indian examples | *Chhota Bheem* (early seasons), *Hanuman* (2005) | *Mahavatar Narsimha*, *Little Singham*, *Motu Patlu* (later seasons) | Ad films, indie shorts |
| Famous global examples | *The Lion King* (1994), Studio Ghibli | *Toy Story*, *KPop Demon Hunters* | *Coraline*, *Wallace & Gromit* |

**2.5D / hybrid:** Many modern productions mix the two. *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* and *KPop Demon Hunters* use 3D with 2D-style shading and line work.

## 4. Styles of 3D animation

| Style | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Stylised / cartoon** | Exaggerated proportions, bright colours | *Motu Patlu*, *Minions* |
| **Photorealistic** | Indistinguishable from real footage | *The Lion King* (2019), VFX creatures in *Brahmastra* |
| **Stylised-realistic** | Realistic lighting with stylised design | *Mahavatar Narsimha*, Pixar films |
| **Toon-shaded / cel-shaded / NPR** | 3D that looks hand-drawn (Non-Photorealistic Rendering) | *Spider-Verse*, *Arcane*, many anime games |
| **Low-poly** | Simple geometric, faceted look | Mobile games, explainer videos |
| **Motion graphics (3D)** | Animated text, logos, abstract shapes | TV channel idents, IPL broadcast graphics |
| **Motion-capture (mocap)** | Real actors' movements recorded and applied to 3D characters | *Kochadaiiyaan* (2014), *Avatar* |

## 5. Where 3D animation is used: applications

### Entertainment
- **Animated feature films**: *Mahavatar Narsimha*, Pixar, DreamWorks
- **TV and OTT series**: *The Legend of Hanuman* (JioHotstar), *Little Singham* (POGO / Discovery Kids)
- **VFX for live-action films**: *Baahubali*, *RRR*, *Brahmastra*, *Kalki 2898 AD*; Hollywood films serviced from India
- **Video games**: characters, environments and cinematics (Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune are gaming hubs)
- **Music videos and concerts**: LED stage visuals

### Advertising and marketing
- TV commercials (product shots of phones, cars, FMCG products like biscuits and shampoo)
- **3D product visualisation** for e-commerce (Flipkart, Myntra, Amazon "view in 3D")
- Social media reels, CGI billboards and "fake out-of-home" (FOOH) ads

### Architecture and real estate (big in Indian cities)
- **Architectural visualisation (ArchViz)**: walkthroughs of apartments before construction. Developers in Bengaluru, Mumbai and Hyderabad use these heavily.
- Interior design previews

### Education and e-learning
- Animated explainers for science and maths (BYJU'S and other ed-tech companies used 3D heavily)
- Virtual labs, anatomy visualisation

### Medical and scientific
- Surgical simulation, drug-mechanism animations, medical device demos
- Scientific visualisation: ISRO mission animations (e.g., Chandrayaan-3 landing simulations shown on news)

### Engineering and manufacturing
- Product design, prototyping and assembly instructions
- **Digital twins**: virtual copies of factories (strong in Bengaluru's engineering-services sector)

### Automotive
- Car configurators and launch films. Indian automakers and the Bengaluru R&D centres of global carmakers use these.

### Legal and forensic
- Accident and crime-scene reconstruction

### Heritage and tourism (high relevance for Karnataka)
- 3D reconstruction of **Hampi**, **Belur–Halebidu**, **Pattadakal** and **Mysuru Palace**
- Virtual museum tours and AR experiences at heritage sites

### AR / VR / XR and the metaverse
- Virtual try-ons (eyewear and jewellery brands), VR training, immersive exhibitions

## 6. Why 3D animation matters for India

- **Rich storytelling source**: Indian mythology, folklore, Panchatantra, Jataka tales and regional legends (Kannada folklore, Yakshagana stories) are ideal for animation. *Mahavatar Narsimha* showed the commercial power of this.
- **Large talent pool**: Indian artists already work on Hollywood blockbusters through studios like DNEG.
- **Government push**: the AVGC-XR national mission, IICT Mumbai and state policies, with Karnataka the first state to have one.
- **Cost advantage plus quality**: India produces world-class work at competitive cost.
- **Huge domestic audience**: kids' TV, OTT platforms and gaming are all growing.
